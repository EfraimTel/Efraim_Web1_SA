Indonesian Heroes Who Can Inspire Young People

General Sudirman

Raden Sudirman also known as General Sudirman was a military officer and the first commander of the Indonesian National Armed Force during the Indonesia National Revolution. Sudirman was borned at 24 January 1916 - 29 January 1950 and later was raised by his uncle. Sudirman's death was mourned throughout Indonesia, with flags flown at half-mast and thousands gathering to see his funeral convoy and procession. His last words are "Take care of the children. Please guide me through the tahlil prayer."

Before he become a commander, he was a teacher. After he dropped out after teacher's college in 1936, he began working as a teacher, and later a headmaster. He was chosen at an election to decide the military's commander-in-chief in Yogyakarta, Sudirman was chosen over Oerip Soemohardjo in a Close vote. His heroic journey to lead the Indonesian taught us about a good leadership.

B.J. Habibie

Bacharuddin Jusuf Habibie also known as B.J. Habibie was a statesman, engineer, scientist and inventor who served as the third president of Indonesia. He was borned 25 June 1936 – 11 September 2019 in Parepare, South Sulawesi. He was the president who had the shortest term in Indonesia. After his death the goverment and neighboring countries specifically Malaysia and East Timor offered condolences, honoring his role in steering Indonesia.

Before becoming president Habibie went to Delft, the Netherlands, to study aviation and aerospace at the Technische Hogeschool Delft. In January 1998 he accepted a nomination for a seventh term as president. On 21 May the institution succeeded BJ Habibie as the next president replacing Suharto. During his presidency term B.J. Habibie had to sacrifice his career as finnancialist in german to save the economy in his country.

Bung Tomo

Sutomo also known as Bung Tomo was famously known as a military leader. He was borned 3 October 1920 – 7 October 1981. He is the military leader who led the Indonesian forces to fight off the British Forces in Surabaya at 10 November 1945. His famous line was "Merdeka Atau Mati" which means Freedom or Death.

During the japanese occupation he work as the official news agency of Japan. In 1944, Sutomo was chosen as a member of the Japanese-sponsored Gerakan Rakyat Baru (New People's Movement) and officer of Pemuda Republik Indonesia (Youth of Indonesian Republic). He is also famous for his great speech for the battle of surabaya to motivate Indonesian forces to fight for the country. His inspiration show a great leadership.
